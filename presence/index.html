Presence is a free side-panel plugin for Jetski and Google Antigravity. It reads your agent's replies out loud in natural HD voice, shows a live talking avatar while it speaks, and lets you talk back hands-free.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add Presence

    Install it from the plugin marketplace in Jetski or Google Antigravity. It opens in your side panel.

  2. 2

    Sign in with Google

    Pick a Google Cloud project. The avatar's voice and video run on Vertex AI in that project.

  3. 3

    Say “Hi Maya”

    Talk or type. Your agent does the work, and Presence tells you about it.

Why Presence asks for Google Cloud access

When you choose Sign in with Google, Presence asks for two things:

  • Your email address, so it can show which Google account is connected.
  • Google Cloud access (the cloud-platform permission), so it can, on your behalf and in your own project:
    • list the Google Cloud projects you can use, so you can pick one;
    • check whether the Vertex AI API, billing, and your permissions are ready;
    • only after you click to confirm: enable the Vertex AI API, link a billing account, grant your account the Vertex AI User role, or create a new project;
    • start Gemini Live sessions on Vertex AI in that project to generate the avatar's voice and video.
💻

Runs on your computer. There is no Presence server.

🔐

Your sign-in stays on your machine. Requests go straight from your computer to Google.

🧾

Billed to your project. Any usage is billed to the Google Cloud project you choose.

See the Privacy Policy for details.

Disconnecting

Use Sign out (or Disconnect) in Presence settings. This deletes the saved sign-in from your computer and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove Presence's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
